# Research audit — 9 November 2026

This is an audit of selected source groups, not verification that every listed location is operating. No calls or site visits were made.

## Corrections made

The previous MBPP source URL no longer opened. The 17 existing council records now link to the [MBPP 2024 archive](https://www.mbpp.gov.my/ms/medias/arkib/arkib-berita/89-berita-2024/96-jabatan-khidmat-pengurusan/810-lokasi-kitar-semula); their names and coordinates were compared with that table. The previous import omitted Batu Lancang and Pasar Tanjung Bungah, which have now been added. The 2024 table also lists Udini Square and Mount Erskine, but those are not added as active because the operator has since reported closure or relocation. Karpal Singh was already present; its council-listed venue coordinate has been added as approximate, not an independently checked destination pin.

Three operator records for New World Park, Setia Spice and Straits Quay were duplicates of council-listed venues. Their original records remain in the JSON with a duplicate status and a link to the retained record, so they no longer inflate the live list. Similarity alone is not enough to merge other records: Tanjung Bungah and Midlands entries need site-level confirmation.

The [2026 Penang Green Council EcoChamps programme](https://www.pgc.com.my/en/penang-green-citizens/i-am-ecochamps-penang-recycling-competition/) describes accepted and excluded materials and names selected Riiicycle stations. Its material categories were attached only to matching named stations, with the programme cited separately and restrictions preserved. They are programme information, not a guarantee of current acceptance. The Riiicycle [operator page](https://www.riiicycle.com/recycling-stations) supplies listings and schedules, which can change.

## Limits and remaining work

The 2024 council table is historical evidence, not proof of current operation. The RECYCLEnCASH operator calls its facilities deployed and supplies coordinates and device IDs, but individual sites, access and item restrictions have not been independently verified. The MAMPAN transcriptions, Pink Bins, e-waste bins and other records still need record-by-record checks. Some coordinates are absent; some source URLs point to entire directory pages rather than individual records. Potential duplicates and the difference between separate devices and separate sites need further review. No claim of complete coverage or physical verification is justified.

Directions remain disabled for source-listed candidates. Source-reported material categories are for discovery only; users should confirm the exact item and current collection arrangements with the operator.
